Sonali Patel is a Canadian designer whose work bridges speculative design, healthcare, robotics, and the evolving relationship between humans, technology, and connection. Studying Innovation Design Engineering at the Royal College of Art & Imperial College, she approaches design as a means of solving problems and shaping possibilities, focusing on how things can develop in conscious and meaningful ways.

Influenced by literature, conceptual art, her own travels, and the places she inhabits, Sonali’s designs embrace the beauty of complexity and the potential within uncharted territory. She is captivated by the act of exploring chaos, letting herself be carried away by the untraditional.
